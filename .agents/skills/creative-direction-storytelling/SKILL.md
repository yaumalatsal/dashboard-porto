# Creative Direction & Storytelling Skill Guide

## Narrative Architecture
1. **Chapter Structure**:
   - **Passage 00 / Arrival** (Hero): Pinned WebGL scene, "FORGING DIGITAL REALMS" statement, coordinates HUD.
   - **Passage 01 / Architect** (About): Systems story, practice map atlas, key metrics.
   - **Passage 02 / Selected Journeys** (Works): Pinned 3D card unfold portal with multi-layer project chapters.
   - **Passage 03 / Craft Ledger** (Skills): Structured capability ledger detailing technology stack.
   - **Passage 04 / Open Gate** (Contact): Cinematic dark walnut section for contrast before footer.

2. **Contrast & Rhythm**:
   - Section background alternating: Warm Cream (`#FAF7F0`) → Parchment (`#F5F0E8`) → Dark Walnut (`#2C2418`).
   - The dark walnut contrast section creates a "lights dimming" effect before the finale.

3. **Editorial Typography**:
   - High contrast between large serif display headings (`Cinzel`) and wide-tracked monospace HUD markers (`JetBrains Mono`).
   - Italicized emphasis text (`<em>`) in amber/gold for key narrative beats.
